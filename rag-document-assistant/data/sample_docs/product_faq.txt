Product FAQ: CloudSync Pro

What is CloudSync Pro?
CloudSync Pro is a file synchronization service that keeps your files updated across all your devices in real time. It supports Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android.

How much storage do I get?
The Basic plan includes 100GB of storage for $9.99/month. The Pro plan includes 1TB for $19.99/month. The Business plan includes 5TB per user for $29.99/month per user, billed annually.

Can I share files with people outside my organization?
Yes. You can generate a shareable link with optional password protection and expiration dates. External sharing can be disabled entirely by workspace admins for Business plan customers.

What happens if I exceed my storage limit?
You will receive an email notification at 90% and 100% usage. File uploads will be paused until you free up space or upgrade your plan, but no existing files will be deleted.

Is my data encrypted?
Yes. All files are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 encryption. Business plan customers can additionally bring their own encryption keys (BYOK).

How do I recover a deleted file?
Deleted files are moved to a Trash folder and retained for 30 days on the Basic and Pro plans, and 180 days on the Business plan. Files can be restored from the Trash at any time within the retention window.

Does CloudSync Pro offer an API?
Yes, a REST API is available on the Pro and Business plans, with rate limits of 1,000 requests per hour on Pro and 10,000 requests per hour on Business.

What is the refund policy?
New subscribers can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase. Refunds are not available for renewal charges after the initial 14-day window.
