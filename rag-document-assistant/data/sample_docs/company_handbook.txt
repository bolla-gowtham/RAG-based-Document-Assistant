Company Handbook: Remote Work Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible to work remotely. Remote work arrangements must be approved by the employee's direct manager and documented in the HR system.

2. Work Hours
Remote employees are expected to be available during core hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their local time zone. Outside of core hours, employees may set their own schedule as long as they complete 40 hours per week and attend all scheduled meetings.

3. Equipment
The company provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $500 stipend for home office setup. Employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection of at least 25 Mbps.

4. Communication Expectations
Remote employees must respond to Slack messages within 2 hours during work hours and join their team's daily standup call. Cameras should be on for all client-facing meetings.

5. Performance Reviews
Remote employees are evaluated using the same criteria as in-office employees: quarterly OKRs, peer feedback, and manager assessment. There is no penalty for remote status in promotion decisions.

6. Security Requirements
All remote employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems and enable two-factor authentication on all company accounts. Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled at all times.

7. Travel Policy
Remote employees are required to attend in-person team offsites twice per year. Travel expenses for offsites are fully covered by the company, including flights, hotel, and meals up to $75/day.
